Guyanonymous' Wildcards 2027 - Changelog (current: v1.0)
=======================================================

Version 1.0 (2027 edition)
--------------------------
Why a new version 1.0
Version 1.0 is a redesign of the structure of the wildcard system, with content fixes. New content comes in the versions after it.

The old pack (up to v133) kept all 188,730 of its lists in one folder. At that size it had grown too heavy to keep updating: every change meant reading and checking that one huge folder, which took a great deal of computer power and time. It also needed better organisation, structure and rules.

Version 1.0 keeps the lists, fixed, and sorts them into modules:
- A core of three (People, Clothing, Helpers) and 15 add-ons. Each add-on needs only the core, so you install only what you want.
- Each module's lists sit in plain, named folders, so you can find a list by browsing, and each module has its own README, catalog PDF and version number.
- Each module is checked and updated on its own, with its own rules, so updates are quicker and safer.
- 18+ material is kept in its own modules (Adult, Medical Graphic).

Guyanonymous' Wildcards 2027 is made to be more future-proof and expandable, and easier to use. Its home, for every 2027 version, is https://github.com/Guyanonymous-x/guyanonymous_wildcards_2027. The old pack (up to v133), the former line of releases that Wildcards 2027 replaces, and its changelog stay at https://github.com/Guyanonymous-x/guyanonymous_wildcards.

From here on, v1.0 is the first version: later changelogs go back only to v1.0.

* A new start: Guyanonymous' Wildcards 2027 v1.0 replaces the old one-folder pack. The library now comes as 18 zips: the core (People, Clothing, Helpers, always installed together) and 15 add-ons that each need only the core, never another add-on: Everyday Places, Objects, Faiths and Festivals, Jobs and Life, Medical, Medical Graphic (18+), Lairs, Odd Places, Vice, Genres and Fantasy, Toys, Games and Amusements, Vehicles, Weapons, Adult (18+) and Animals and Nature. Each zip has its own version, README and catalog PDF, and its lists sit in plain, named folders. 197,757 lists in all.
* Medical split at PG-13: Medical holds the places and routine, draped exams at PG-13 and below; Medical Graphic (18+) holds intimate exams, surgery in progress and childbirth. Pregnancy moved to People.
* Vehicles and Weapons are now two modules; Lairs, Odd Places and Vice are modules of their own. Adult and Medical Graphic got plain folder names (Adult's six levels run from l1_suggestive to l6_climax_and_groups).
* Ages: 14 new age bands.
* Gender diverse people: 146 detail lists (binders, top-surgery scars and more) wired into 205 makers, with binder and scar copies in the body lists; a trans woman chain of lists; chest hair for trans men.
* Names: anyone_names, 257 gender-open names from 23 regions, and the openings that use it; names for everyone described in a scene; fixed names in cast lines.
* Dressed people at medium and long length: 731 dressed makers with 468 medium body pieces, pickers, group wrappers and _traits start lists. Period clothing now dresses people (38 period person prompts). The 202 pi_ interaction scenes moved to Clothing.
* Skin: tan and sunburn matched to the skin tone (9 lists).
* Shrunken people: their pieces grew from 4 to 12 lines, and the people are named.
* Pregnancy: after-birth and topless makers; the nude after-birth lists moved to Medical Graphic.
* Nudity: nude detail makers; the nude man makers now say plainly what is visible (penis and scrotum, small and relaxed), with _scene copies; semi-erect and after-sex penis pieces moved to Adult.
* Wounds: the wound lists were rebuilt in visual words, with places matched to each wound.
* Ages in visual words: age summaries describe what is seen.
* Fixes: leftover "adult" labels and gaps; the old index lists retired.
