They hold the shape of the position called utpiditaka. Malik lies on his back with both knees bent and drawn up, his feet held flat against Jae-won's chest, while Jae-won kneels and leans in, Jae-won's hips pressed close against Malik's. Jae-won holds Malik's feet against his chest with both hands, and Malik's knees fold close to his own shoulders.
