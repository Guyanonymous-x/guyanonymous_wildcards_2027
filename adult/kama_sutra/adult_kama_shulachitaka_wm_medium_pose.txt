They hold the shape of the position called shulachitaka. Wanjiru lies on her back with one leg raised high so her heel rests on Baraka's forehead, the other leg stretched out flat, Baraka's hips pressed close against Wanjiru's. Wanjiru's raised leg is straight and steady, Baraka's head tipped back a little against the heel, and Baraka's hands hold Wanjiru's hips.
