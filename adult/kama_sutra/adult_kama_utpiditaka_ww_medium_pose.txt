They hold the shape of the position called utpiditaka. Imani lies on her back with both knees bent and drawn up, her feet held flat against Soo-jin's chest, while Soo-jin kneels and leans in, Soo-jin's hips pressed close against Imani's. Soo-jin holds Imani's feet against her chest with both hands, and Imani's knees fold close to her own shoulders.
